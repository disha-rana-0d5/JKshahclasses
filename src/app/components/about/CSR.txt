Hansaben Kantilal Shah & Radha Ramakrishna Baliga Charitable Trust (HKSRRB)
Mission Statement:
At HKSRRB Trust, our mission is to alleviate hunger and poverty across India. We are committed to providing sustenance to those in need and nurturing a society where no one goes to sleep hungry.
Vision Statement:
Our vision is a hunger-free India, where every citizen has access to nutritious meals. We aspire to create a network of support that extends from compassionate individuals to socially responsible corporates, all working together to eradicate hunger from our nation.
HKSRRB Charitable Trust was set up in 2010, by Mr. J. K. Shah, a distinguished Chartered Accountant, successful entrepreneur and founder, J. K. Shah Classes; and his wife, CA Purnima J. Shah. Mrs. Shah also served as CFO at J. K. Shah Classes since inception till 2022.
Since commencing activities in 2013, the trust has grown from supporting 7 families to 700 families in Mumbai by providing monthly groceries. However, our journey doesn’t stop there; we aim to extend our reach, touching lives of countless more.
